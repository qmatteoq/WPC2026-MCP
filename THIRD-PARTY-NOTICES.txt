The color.png and outline.png icons in appPackage/ are from:
https://github.com/microsoft/copilot-camp/tree/937fd5b6af4c705597381dd24d5e8aa7693521ad/src/cowork/zava-claims-sso

The Zava Insurance scenario and packaging approach are inspired by Copilot Camp
labs CWRK02, CWRK03, and E10. This sample implements a smaller, read-only,
file-backed MCP server rather than copying the lab's Azure Storage or DCR code.
It is a session sample, not an official Microsoft product or endorsed deployment.
